Theorem 43. Continuity Principles.

The following principles describe the continuity of various functions:
🔗
  1. Any constant function is continuous.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Any polynomial function is continuous.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. Trigonometric functions and their inverse trigonometric functions (such as \(\sin\text{,}\) \(\cos\text{,}\) and \(\tan\)) are continuous on their domains.
    🔗
    🔗
  4. Exponential functions and logarithmic functions are continuous on their domains.
    🔗
    🔗
  5. For any positive integer \(n\) and any continuous function \(f\text{,}\) the functions \([f(x)]^n\) and \(\sqrt[n]{f(x)}\) are continuous. When \(n\) is even, the domain of \(\sqrt[n]{f(x)}\) is restricted to values of \(x\) for which \(f(x) \geq 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  6. If \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) are continuous, then their sum \(f(x) + g(x)\text{,}\) difference \(f(x) - g(x)\text{,}\) and product \(f(x) \cdot g(x)\) are also continuous.
    🔗
    🔗
  7. If \(f(x)\) and \(g(x)\) are continuous, then the quotient \(\frac{g(x)}{f(x)}\) is continuous for all \(x\) such that \(f(x) \neq 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  8. If \(g(x)\) is continuous at \(x=c\) with \(\lim_{x \to c} g(x) = b\text{,}\) and \(f(x)\) is continuous at \(x=b\text{,}\) then the composite function \((f \circ g)(x) = f(g(x))\) is continuous at \(x=c\text{.}\) That is:
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x \to c} (f \circ g)(x) = f(b) = f\left( \lim_{x \to c} g(x) \right) \end{equation*}
    In fact, if \(g\) is continuous at \(c\) and \(f\) is continuous at \(g(c)\text{,}\) then the composite function \(f \circ g\) is continuous at \(x=c\text{.}\)
    🔗
